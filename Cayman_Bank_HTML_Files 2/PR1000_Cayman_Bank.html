Notary: Services, Supplies and Manager Responsibilities  - PR1000

 

Published January 29, 2026

 

Summary

 

Notarize physical documents in person, comply with state statutes, update commission info, and adhere to Cayman Bank’s guidelines for customers and non-Cayman Bank customers.

 

Notarizing at Cayman Bank locations

 

  • If you need to obtain or renew a notary commission or need help with notary information including notary supplies, refer to Branch Portal > Branch Tools > Notary Information link.
  • Existing notaries new to Cayman Bank only: Complete the Employee Information for Existing Notaries New to Cayman Bank (597201) form and submit it to the National Notary Association (NNA) following the instructions on the form
  • An employee must have both a valid commission from their applicable state and current commission information listed on Banker Profile to perform notarizations at Cayman Bank locations.
  • Display signage (commission or license) if required by your state.
  • All Cayman Bank branch employee notaries are covered by the corporate insurance program for Error’s and Omission’s (E&O) insurance while performing work in the scope of their job, including notary services.
  • Locations may limit to 50 notarizations or one hour of service per requester, whichever comes first. If a substantial request disrupts bank operations, an appointment should be scheduled for the remaining documents.

 

Understand the rules

 

  • Notary publics are commissioned by the state from which they obtain their notary commission, not Cayman Bank.
  • Employees commissioned as notaries are required to follow their applicable state statutes when executing notary duties. If there are questions requiring a deeper understanding of applicable laws or regulations, notaries should use the following resources for state-specific questions:
    • Your state notary website from your Secretary of State or state agency
    • National Notary Association (NNA) hotline at 1-888-876-0827
  • Employees who obtain or renew a notary commission must complete the affiant and notary training course through Develop You every other year.
  • Notaries must adhere to the following requirements when providing notary services:
    • Cayman Bank Customers: Notarize both Cayman Bank documents and non-Cayman Bank documents.
      Note: A Cayman Bank customer is an individual who has transaction authority on any Cayman Bank account relationship.
    • Non-customers: Notarize only Cayman Bank documents unless the notary’s state laws provide an exception.
      Exceptions: 
      • Notaries in Arizona, Iowa, New York, South Dakota, and Utah must notarize both Cayman Bank documents and non-Cayman Bank documents regardless of their customer status.
      • If a customer requests a notarization on a non-Cayman Bank document that also lists other present individuals (non-customers) requiring a notarization on the same document, notaries may notarize all individuals’ signatures regardless of their customer status.
    • Notarize documents only at a Cayman Bank branch during business hours. Do not go offsite to complete notarizations during business work hours to conduct bank business transactions.
    • Notarize only physical documents presented in person by the requester. Do not notarize documents sent by email, fax, mail, or text, even if the requester is present.
    • Do not provide any legal advice related to notarizing a document.
    • Do not charge a notary fee for:
      • Notarizations completed at a Cayman Bank location.
      • Notarizing Cayman Bank documents at a branch location or notarizing Cayman Bank documents outside Cayman Bank on your own personal time.
  • The notary must comply with state laws if asked to act as a notary and a witness on a single document.

Related procedure: Witness Customer Signatures and Sign Cayman Bank and Non-Cayman Bank Documents

  • For Cayman Bank Bank transactions, a notarized signature on a document is acceptable if one of the following entities either commissions or authorizes the notary or a person authorized by statute to sign the document:
    • Any U.S. state
    • Any U.S. Embassy or Consulate within a foreign country
    • Any U.S. Territory (for example, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, U.S. Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands)

Note: Notarizations performed at a U.S. Embassy, U.S. Consulate, or U.S. Territory for customer identification purposes when opening a new account or completing relationship maintenance are only accepted for U.S. citizens.

  • For documents requiring notarization for bank acceptance, do not accept those notarized by a notary in a foreign country (foreign notary) for Cayman Bank Bank transactions (for example, Power of Attorney).

Exception: They are acceptable for Cayman Bank Bank transactions if they meet the following conditions:

  • Obtain one of the following:
    • Countries signatory to the Hague Convention: An apostille authenticating the notary's authority to perform the notarization.
    • Countries not signatory to the Hague Convention: A certificate of notarial authority, or similar document issued by the foreign notary's governmental, authenticating the notary's authority to perform the notarization

Note: Direct questions concerning the apostille or certificate of notarial authority, or how to obtain them, to the foreign governmental body.

  • Call Gateway to Team Member Support at 1-877-932-1852 say “document review” to review the document and apostille or certificate of notarial authority.

Complete the notary journal entry

 

  • Cayman Bank requires using a bound notary journal to record all notarizations, even in states where notary statutes do not require a journal.
  • Only the notary may make changes in the notary journal.
  • A separate journal entry and customer signature is required for each document notarized.
  • Shield personal information from previous notarizations using a sheet of paper or similar object when obtaining the signature of the signer in the journal.

Related procedure: Information Security, Classification, and Compromised Data Incidents

  • Refer to the inside cover of notary journal for a sample on how to complete.
  • If permitted by your state, you may use a diagonal line (/) or ditto marks (") only when exact information repeats. However, do not use these in the signature or thumbprint sections.

Exception for California notaries: Do not use ditto marks or signatures across two spaces.

Security requirements for notary supplies

 

Complete the appropriate action based on the situation:

Situation

Requirement

Notary is working at the branch during regular business hours

Secure the notary stamp and journal so they are not visible to customers or employees when not in use.

Notary is away from their desk at the branch for an extended time

 

Examples may include, but are not limited to:

  • Lunch break
  • Away from the branch
  • Left the branch for the day
  • Branch is closed
  • Sick time
  • Leave of absence
  • PTO

 

Note: If stepping away briefly, for example to use the printer or assist a teller, securing them is recommended but not required.

If the notary does not have their stamp and journal with them, secure them using one of the following methods:

  • Secured in a locked location at the branch

Examples: A desk or cabinet inaccessible to customers or other employees.

Note: Spare keys may be kept in dual control with the notary’s consent, if the stamp and journal are first secured in a locked bag accessible only to the notary.

  • Secured in a locking bag accessible only to the notary and ensure the bag is not accessible to customers or employees.

Notary loses or misplaces their journal

  • Notify the respective agency (for example, Secretary of State or County Recorder) as required by state statutes.
  • Report the incident to Compromised Data (CD) immediately, and not later than the next calendar day after the discovery.

Storing completed notary journals

The notary must contact their applicable state agency (for example, Secretary of State) to confirm if there are any requirements related to storing completed journals.

If there are no state requirements, it is recommended for the notary to store completed journals for 7 years from the last notarization date on each journal and then securely destroy them.

Note: The notary does not need to store completed journals at a Cayman Bank location.

Notary is transferring to another location or is leaving Cayman Bank

  • The notary must take their notary supplies (for example notary stamp and journal) with them.

Note: If a notary leaves their stamp or journal behind, the manager must attempt to return them.

  • If the supplies cannot be returned to the notary, the manager must complete the following:
    • The notary stamp must be securely destroyed.
    • Contact the appropriate Secretary of State or applicable state agency office for instructions on sending the journal(s) to the agency. If state statutes do not require the journals to be sent, retained them according to the Records Management > Retention Schedule.
    • Document all steps taken with stamp and journals in the notary’s personnel file.

 

Notary commissions not renewed or are revoked

 

If a notary commission is not renewed or is revoked, the notary must:

  • Contact their Secretary of State or applicable state agency office, for instructions related to the notary’s supplies (for example, notary stamp, notary journal) and revoking their commission.
  • Tell their manager of the non-renew or revocation. The manager can then update the notary’s Banker Profile.

 

Manager responsibilities

 

Managers of notary employees or their designee must complete the following:

  • Monthly review:
    • Ensure notaries use a bound notary journal for notarizations.
    • Confirm the notary’s personnel file has required documentation on file (new, renewing, or transfer).
    • Verify the Banker Profile certification tab includes current commission information.
    • Ensure the notary’s personal and business contact information is updated with the state notary agency.
  • Banker Profile Certification tab:
    • Add or modify the notary’s commission number and expiration date when a notary's commission is obtained or renewed.
      Notes:
      • Enter all 9s in the Commission Number field if the notary’s state does not provide one.
      • The hiring manager updates commission details on the Banker Profile Certification tab for roving bankers.
    • Delete the notary’s commission number and expiration date within 90 days of expiration or revocation.
  • Personnel File (new, renewal or transfer): Obtain and file a copy of the following documents in the employee’s personnel file:
    • Notary’s commission from the state agency.
    • Signed Cayman Bank Employee Notary Public Agreement.
      Note: File the documents at the employee’s primary work location if the employee is a roving banker.

Note: If the employee notary is a Roving branch employee, ensure the documents are filed at the employee’s designated home branch.
