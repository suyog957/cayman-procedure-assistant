Published October 10, 2025

 

Summary

 

Process for storing and shipping records and destruction of materials and inactive records.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

 

Understand the rules

 

 

Use secure destruction to protect the confidentiality of bank and customer information.

  • Shred vendors or secure-destruction services:
    • Branches must use only the approved shred vendor or secure-destruction service selected for their region, or an approved in-location, cross-cut shredder.
    • Management must ensure the approved vendor or service provides locking, secure-destruction bins or similar service containers for securely destroying dry trash.
    • Do not remove documents or items from vendor-provided, secure-destruction bins.

Exception: In urgent situations, submit a Container Access Request to open the bin on Shred Services Order Forms. If dispatched, the vendor may submit additional charges.

  • Secure-destruction bin keys:
    • Retained by Shred Services, not by the branch.
    • Cannot be assigned to, or in the possession of, employees.
    • Do not give to contract employees or janitorial services.
  • Ensure secure destruction bins are:
    • Not accessible to customers.
    • Always locked and secured or under camera coverage within a Cayman Bank location (not in lobby areas).
    • Chained or locked in the back of the retail location if the branch is without space for the bin.
    • Not used for wet trash disposal (for example, break room and bathroom trash).
    • Not overfilled where items can easily be removed.

Note: If the bin is full and must be emptied before the scheduled pickup, submit an Off-Schedule Service Request on Shred Services Order Forms.

 

 

 

ATM items and teller work

 

 

  1. Identify and remove teller work and ATM batches stored 31 calendar days or more.
    • Teller work and ATM items are stored for a minimum of 30 calendar days; do not remove or destroy before that timeframe.
    • Destroy items stored 31 calendar days or more monthly, according to the Teller/ATM Work Destruction Control Online task or more frequently, for example:
      • Daily, as items for the current day are added to the check tray (newest in and oldest out)
      • Weekly, on a set day
  2. Review the items to be destroyed to ensure they do not include:
    • Teller work and ATM batches stored 30 calendar days or less
    • Any items other than teller work and ATM batches
  3. Remove any rubber bands, paper clips, or binders from the bundles.
  4. Place the items and batch header tickets in a secure destruction bin.

Inactive records

 

 

  • The AU manager must ensure implementation of the Regional Banking Records Management program and training of all employees on the requirements. This responsibility includes the maintenance, accessibility, and final disposition of the record for its entire lifecycle.
  • All records created by an employee are the exclusive property of Cayman Bank and are not the property of the record's author, creator, or custodian. Unless otherwise provided by law, no employee has any personal or property right to any Cayman Bank records, including records that they helped create.
    • Retain, store, and securely destroy all records according to the Records Management > Retention Schedule. Secure information during the retention and storage phases of the life cycle.
    • Retain and store records if Cayman Bank owned. Records owned by a client or customer must not be stored.
    • Retain records in a form that accurately reflects the purpose and content of the record (for example, paper, Adobe PDF).
    • Maintain records in a form that allows for accurate reproduction for later reference as needed.
    • Suspend destruction of all records listed on a Record Preservation Hold Order until the Cayman Bank Legal Department releases the Record Preservation Hold.
      • The Cayman Bank Legal Department is the only department with the authority to issue or release a Record Preservation Hold Order.
      • The original required retention period applies to records affected after the release of a Legal Hold Order.

Refer to the Release a hold subsection of the Add, modify, or release a record hold section.

Complete the appropriate action based on the situation:

Situation

Action

Records stored at Iron Mountain

  • Annually, ship prior year inactive records, eligible for retention, to Iron Mountain within one year after becoming inactive.
  • Current year inactive records are not eligible for destruction, except administrative (ADM100) documents retained for less than one year according to the Records Management > Retention Schedule.
  • Iron Mountain is responsible for the destruction of records and sends a confirmation Management Risk (IMR) semi-annually.

Records stored onsite

  1. Branch management must perform a full inspection of all spaces (for example, cabinets, desks, unused storage areas) where records could be stored in the branch in order to locate all records eligible for destruction.

  3. Securely destroy drafts, copies, and duplicate receipts (multiply) after they have met their business purpose. Do not retain more than 3 years.
  4. Review and destroy inactive records quarterly, as prompted through Control Online.
  5. Securely destroy records after they have met their business purpose. Approvals are not required before destruction.

Paper documents

 

 

Place all paper documents labeled public, internal use, confidential, or restricted in a secure destruction bin designated for paper each night.

  • Render the following items unusable before placing them in a secure destruction bin:
    • Unused negotiable items (for example, official bank checks, new account starter sheets)
    • Consumer credit reports and FICO score information
  • Protect the confidentiality of bank and customer information using secure destruction.
  • Securely destroy unofficial records stored onsite with an expired business need.
  • The branch manager may assign one employee to collect confidential information from each teller station, banker’s desk, customer service areas, and branch-serviced ATM areas.
  • Do not accept non-Cayman Bank paper documents containing confidential information from customers.

Prepare and ship records

 

 

  • All records stored onsite must be stored in an area that is inaccessible to the public and third-party vendors, utilizing any of the following storage options:
    • Secured box
    • File cabinet
    • Shelves
    • Drawers
  • All records should be stored together in the same area if possible.
  • Current year records are stored onsite.
  • A designated employee must perform a full inspection of all spaces (for example, cabinets, desks, unused storage areas) where records could be stored in the branch in order to locate all previous year inactive records.
  • Control Online annual indexing task: Assigned to branch management only. Ship previous year inactive records to Iron Mountain storage annually. Do not store onsite.
  • Ship previous year inactive records to Iron Mountain storage annually. Do not store onsite.
  • Complete shipment of inactive records from the prior year by September 30 annually. Inactive records shipping window is April 1 through September 30.

 

 

 

 

Prepare records for storage

 

 

If you have records with historical value, access Cayman Bank Corporate Archives for determining historical value guidelines and how to store these records appropriately.

  1. Obtain a Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) box.
    • Files and envelopes are not accepted.
    • Do not add additional barcodes to these boxes.
    • RFID boxes can be ordered through the Order Supplies option in Security Control > Records tab.
  2. Sort and organize inactive records in the following order:
    • Record class code
      Note: Each box can only contain one record class code.
      Reference: Records Management > Retention Schedule
    • Calendar year
      Note: AUD200 and BNK460 can only include one calendar year per box.
    • Retrieval method (for example, alphabetical, date, numeric order) within each class code
  3. Access Security Control, select the Records tab, and complete the appropriate actions based on the situation:

Situation

Action

Record Storage Box Index populates with the applicable class code and year

  1. Expand the applicable Record Class Code Index.
  2. Review and update the index if needed.
  3. Verify the full alphanumeric number from the blue pre-affixed barcode in the Barcode/SKP# field matches the numbers on the box (for example, RF123456789).

Record Storage Box Index does not populate with the applicable class code and year

Complete the following actions to create a new Records Storage Box Index:

  1. Select Manage Record.
  2. Select Create a Record Storage Box Index from the menu and complete the fields.
  3. Type the full alphanumeric number from the blue pre-affixed barcode in the Barcode/SKP# field based on the box type (for example, RF123456789).
  1. Select Submit.
  2. Select the Records tab, expand the applicable class code to locate the completed Record Storage Box Index, and print.
  3. Place the copy of the Record Storage Box Index with the record.
  4. Pack each record class code set of records in a separate box using the following guidelines:
    • Pack the box to a maximum capacity of 90%, leaving 1½ inches of free space behind the last file.
    • If the box is less than 90% full, fill the box to 90% capacity with packing material (for example, old newspapers, packing filler).
    • Pack the box using the retrieval order method selected above (for example, alphabetical, date, numeric order).
    • Pack each file in the same direction with the files facing in the appropriate direction according to paper size.
      • Letter-size paper: Face files toward the end of the box with the outside label.
      • Legal-size paper: Face files toward the longest side of the box.
  5. Ensure records are stored appropriately until sent to Iron Mountain.

Note: Do not seal the box until it is time to request the box to be sent to Iron Mountain.

Add, modify, or release a record hold

 

 

Process an order to add, modify, or release a hold on a record at Iron Mountain.

 

 

 

Add a hold

 

 

  1. Review the order for a listing of affected records.
  2. Access Security Control and select the Records tab to locate the index forms for the boxes identified in the order.
  3. Access the EIS-Information Management RC Listing on the Information Management website and open the Record Coordinator List Excel document.
  4. Select the Consumer Small Business Banking tab at the bottom of the document.
  5. Scroll right to find the name of the Group Records Coordinator.
  6. Prepare and send an email to the Group Records Coordinator.
    • Subject line: New Request to Add a Hold On a Record
    • Include the following information:
      • A copy of the Order for the hold
      • A list of the records, including the SKP barcode numbers, where a hold should be placed
      • Your branch information located in Security Control:
        • Customer ID
        • Department ID
        • Division ID
        • Bar code number (if only specific boxes are to be on hold and not the entire department)

Note: If the records or boxes identified in the order cannot be located, email the Information Management Office (IMO) and provide a detailed list of the records and follow IMO’s directions.

  1. Follow additional instructions within the Order and document the actions taken.
  2. File the order in your location's record-retention file. 

 

 

 

Modify an existing hold

 

 

  1. Review the Order to modify an existing hold on a record.
  2. Access the EIS-Information Management RC Listing on the Information Management website and open the Record Coordinator List Excel document.
  3. Select the Consumer Small Business Banking tab at the bottom of the document.
  4. Scroll right to find the name of the Group Records Coordinator.
  5. Prepare and send an email to the Group Records Coordinator (GRC).

The GRC will notify the Information Management Office (IMO) there is a modification to an existing hold.

  • Subject line: Request Modification to an Existing Hold On a Record
  • Include a copy of the Hold Modification Notice or Order.

Note: Types of modifications may include, but not limited to:

  • Add boxes to an existing hold
  • Release boxes from an existing hold
  • Add department(s) to an existing hold
  • Release Departments from an existing hold

 

 

 

Release a hold

 

 

  1. Review the Order to release a hold on a record.
  2. Access the EIS-Information Management RC Listing on the Information Management website and open the Record Coordinator List Excel document.
  3. Select the Consumer Small Business Banking tab at the bottom of the document.
  4. Scroll right to find the name of the Group Records Coordinator.
  5. Prepare and send an email to the Group Records Coordinator (GRC).

The GRC will notify the Information Management Office (IMO) to release the hold for records stored at Iron Mountain.

  • Subject line: Notice to Release Notice or Order
  • Include the following information:
    • Copy of the Hold Release Notice or Order
    • List of records, including the SKP barcode to be released
