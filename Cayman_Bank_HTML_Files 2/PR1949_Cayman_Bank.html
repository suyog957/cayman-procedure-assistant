Savings Bonds: Rate and Redeem - PR1949

 

Published December 17, 2025

 

Summary

 

Rate and redeem full, half-sheet, series E, EE, or I savings bonds, including identifying presenters, resolve scanning issues, remove or reverse bonds, and special handling procedures.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

Understand the rules

 

 

  •       Confirm the presenter is a consumer customer with an open and active Cayman Bank checking, savings, or time account (CD), for 12 months or longer.

Note: If the account has been opened less than 12 months, you may redeem bonds up to $1,000 per day (aggregate redemption value). If the total cash redemption value is more than $1,000, the customer has the following bond redemption options:

  •      Redeem the bonds up to the allowable $1,000 total value and take back the remaining bonds.
  •      Redeem all bonds at full value using the Treasury Direct website.

Exception – Customer eligible for The Private Bank Experience: The $1,000 allowable redemption amount does not apply to customers who are eligible for The Private Bank Experience regardless of how long the account has been opened.

  •       Do not redeem bonds payable to a business or trust.
  •       The U.S. Department of the Treasury prohibits a bank from charging a fee to redeem a U.S. savings bond.
  •       If issued in a foreign country, the death certificate must be the original or a certified copy.
  •       If redeeming multiple bonds within the same transaction, the customer must sign each bond. Basic transaction information can be documented on one bond and is not required to be written on every bond in the transaction.
    •      Any additional required documentation for unique situations as detailed in the procedure below must be written on each bond (for example, name change reason, beneficiary, decedent information).
    •      The following information may be written on only one bond when multiple bonds are presented:
      •      Identification
      •      Social security number (SSN) (if the printed SSN on the front of the bond does not belong to the customer)
      •      Customer's current address
      •      Type of Cayman Bank account
      •      Last 4 digits of the account number
      •      Date the account was opened
  •       Tell the customer when additional time or assistance is required to review the transaction.
  •       Transaction approvers: Introduce yourself to the customer and explain your role when you are asked to review a transaction or request.

Co-owners or multiple payees

  • John Smith or Mary Smith: Names connected by Or indicates either person may endorse and redeem the savings bond.
  • John Smith with Mary Smith: Names connected by with indicates both individuals must endorse and be present to redeem the savings bond.

 

Unacceptable bond registrations: The customer must contact Treasury Direct to redeem the bond for the following examples:

  • Names separated by and or &: John Smith and Mary Smith
  • Names not separated: Mary Jones Tom Smith
  • Names separated by symbol or punctuation:
    • Mary Jones/Tom Smith
    • Mary Jones, Tom Smith
  • Names are stacked:
    Mary Jones
    Tom Smith

Owner with a beneficiary

John A. Smith POD Jane Johnson

The person named first owns the bond and may endorse and redeem it. If the owner dies, the beneficiary automatically becomes the sole owner. The registration says "POD" standing for "PAYABLE ON DEATH."

Rate and redeem

Note: Do not redeem savings bonds payable to a business or Trust. Refer the presenter to Treasury Direct. This procedure is complete.

  1. Access the T521 on TellerView and complete all applicable and required fields on the Bond Detail screens.
    • If the address field prefills, you must verify with the customer the address displayed in the T521 Redeem bond transaction is the mailing address to mail their interest reporting Form 1099 at the end of the calendar year. If not, enter the mailing address provided by the customer before proceeding.

Note: The Form 1099 mailing address does not have to match their current physical address.

  • Tell the customer their interest reporting Form 1099:
    • Is printed on the Teller transaction receipt for the T521 redemption transaction.
    • Will be mailed at the end of the year to the address provided.
  1. Select Send to Pin Pad and ask the customer to confirm the following on the PIN Pad:
    • Last four digits of their tax ID number
    • First and last name
    • Address to mail their Form 1099
  2. Complete the appropriate action based on the bond type:

Bond type

Action

E, EE, or I

Determine the bond values. Do not scan bonds at this time.

  • To value the bond, always use the issue date, which consists of month and year (for example, 5/89 or May 1989). Valid issue dates are:
    • Series E: May 1941 to June 1980
    • Series EE: January 1980 to present
    • Series I: September 1998 to present
  • Do not use the validation date, which consists of month, day, and year (for example, 1/2/89). The validation date could differ if the bond is printed on a date other than the issue date.

    Example: Bond is reprinted due to a name change.

Note: Manually type the MICR line into the system by dropping alpha characters and prefilling with zeros so the entire serial number is 11 numeric characters.

Reverse a transaction with a savings bond

 

 

TellerView typically allows transaction reversals within 1 hour of completion or until the batch is closed for transactions included in a batch, whichever occurs first. After one hour, you cannot return the physical savings bond to the customer because bond processing has begun.

Note: When the bond from a reversed transaction is returned to the customer and has been branded, stamped, or signed, the customer must contact Treasure Direct to redeem the bond.

Related procedure:Search the Electronic Journal and Reverse Teller Transactions

  1. Access the T621 EJ Search.
  2. Search for the applicable transaction (Item Cashing [T100], Deposit [T200] or Purchase Bank Check [T400]) in which the bonds were processed.

Note: The T521 Redeem Bond transaction cannot be reversed in the T621; the funding transaction must be reversed instead. Once the funding transaction is reversed, TellerView will automatically reverse the T521 Redeem Bond transaction.

  1. Once you locate the transaction:
    1. Select the arrow on the left of the screen to expand the transaction.
    2. Select Reverse.
  2. When the system message Redeem Bonds process not completed displays, complete the appropriate action based on the reason for the reversal.

Reversal reason

Action

You processed the wrong funding transaction (T100, T200, or T400).

Example: An item cashing transaction was processed when the customer requested to deposit the bond.

  1. From the drop-down menu, select the correct funding transaction from one of these options:
    • Get cash
    • Deposit
    • Purchase a Bank Check

Note: TellerView will automatically tie the original savings bond (T521) transaction to the corrected funding transaction.

  1. Follow standard procedures to complete the funding transaction.

This procedure is complete.

Customer does not want to proceed with the redemption transaction and requests the bond back

Tell the customer if the transaction is reversed, they must contact Treasury Direct to redeem the bond. The bank will not be able to redeem the bond later since they are branded, stamped, and signed.

  • Customer requests bond back: Select Reverse Redeem Bonds.
  • Customer wants to complete bond redemption: Complete the Rate and redeem section.

    This procedure is complete.

A system error occurred (for example, freeze or timed out)

Select Reverse Redeem Bonds in the drop-down menu.

  1. Securely destroy the printed Redemption Statement from the T521 when you receive the message to do so when the reversal is confirmed complete.
  2. If the reversal is due to reprocessing the T521 due to a system error, complete the Tax Information Reporting Correction, Duplicate or Refund of Backup Withholding Request form to report the situation to ensure accurate tax information reporting. 

 

 

 Resolve scanning issues: Series E, EE, or I

 

 

Complete any of the following actions to resolve scan issues with series E, EE, or I savings bonds:

  •       Gently rub the bond on the edge of a counter, curving the paper, and rescan.
  •       Rotate the bond and scan it upside down.
  •       Place the bond in a clear document carrier and rescan.

Reference: Item scanning - teller reference guide

If the actions do not resolve the issue:

  1. Cancel the original transaction.
  2. On the teller system, access T521 and complete all applicable fields by manually typing the MICR line information on the Bond Detail screen.
  3. When the branding message appears, complete the following actions for each bond in the same order as entered in TellerView.
    1. Obtain a clear document carrier.
    2. Write Bond on a blank piece of paper.
    3. Fold the paper in half and place it in the document carrier, ensuring the word Bond appears in the window.

Note: Do not place the bond in the document carrier before scanning.

  1. Select Rescan to create an image of the document carrier.

Note: If the scanner jams, remove the paper from the document carrier and rescan only the paper. Return the paper to the document carrier.

  1. Place the bonds in the applicable document carrier in the same order used in step 3.

Note: When a savings bond cannot be scanned, it will cause an Exception Processing System (EPS) exception.

  1. Press [Ctrl] + [D] and ask the customer how they want funds disbursed:

Disbursement option

Action

Cash

  1. Select Option 1 – Cash Bonds.

The system navigates to the Cash U.S Bonds transaction.

  1. Select Yes for the message Is this a full or half sheet bond?

Deposit

  1. Select Option 3 – Deposit Bonds.

The system navigates to the Deposit transaction.

  1. Select Half/Full Sheet Bond.

Purchase Bank Check

  1. Select Option 4 – Purchase Bank Check

The system navigates to the Purchase Bank Check transaction.

  1. Select Half/Full Sheet Bond
  1. Continue with the applicable transaction and follow all system prompts.
  2. When the transaction is complete, place the document carrier with the savings bond and deposit ticket (if applicable) in the Teller work bin.
