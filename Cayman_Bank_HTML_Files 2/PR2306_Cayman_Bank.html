Copy Requests: Statements, Debit or Credit Items, and Account Documentation - PR2306

 

Published January 30, 2026

 

Summary

 

Obtain a copy of an account statement including year-end statements, debit, credit, or return item posted to an account. Assist with a request for printed account information. Retrieve document images for checking, savings, safe deposit box, and time accounts.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

Understand the rules

 

  • The customer must have authority to request this transaction.
  • A copy of a previously deposited payment slip is only available for legal purposes (for example, an IRS audit).
  • If the requestor is a non-Prime or Premier Checking account owner requesting a copy of a statement, provide only the pages related to the non-Prime or Premier Checking account owner.
  • If an owner was removed and the account remains open, the former owner can only request copies for  the time period during which they were an owner.
  • Do not:
    • Fax, email, or mail statement copies directly to a third-party (individuals or entities) without transaction authority on the specified account.
      Note: One-time requests to fax statements are not permitted
    • Fax the following statement or transaction information to customers:
      • Copies of statements printed from OIB or MARS
      • SVP Checking or Savings Account History screen prints
      • Form 100, 120, 200, 300, 400, and 500 statements
    • Provide copies of customer statements to Cayman Bank Home Mortgage Consultants (HMCs). Refer HMCs to their current process guides for information on obtaining general account information.

Exception: Customers with disabilities may request to have their account statement faxed to them on a regular or recurring basis. Go to the Maintain Statements procedure for additional information and to complete the request.

Assist with copy requests

 

Branch Resource – DDA Foundational:

  • Obtaining images for an item or transaction can be completed by bankers or BC BPSRs on behalf of bankers.
  • Statements, transactions, letters, and worksheets viewable in MARS can be printed by the banker or emailed immediately.
    • Banker Connection may only email a banker with a Cayman Bank.com address.
    • If the customer wants copies emailed directly to them, the banker may either:
      • Order through OIB View Later option.
      • Advise the customer to order the copy through Cayman Bank Customer Service or Online Banking.

 

  1. Ensure the banker has identified the requestor and verified they have transaction authority.
  • If the requestor is a fiduciary who has not been added to the account (personal representative, trustee, conservator/guardian, attorney-in-fact, representative payee), ensure that the appropriate documentation and review requirements have been met.
  • If an owner was removed and the account remains open, the former owner can only request copies for the time period during which they were an owner.
  • Select the appropriate situation from Special Accounts and Relationships - CD/DDA Complex.
    1. Ask the banker May I walk you through the process of ordering the copy, or would you prefer I do it for you?
  • Walk the banker through ordering: Use existing procedures to assist the banker with OIB or MARS. Go to step 3.

If they are unable to pull images in OIB or MARS, they should verify their access with their management.

  • Order on the banker’s behalf: Go to the next step.
    1. Complete the action based on the situation:

Transaction and offsets

Go to the Obtain an account debit, credit, or returned item section.

 

Refer to the Search for Item or Transaction procedure if needed to locate transaction details.

 

  • View Now: A digital item may be viewed and downloaded immediately.
    • On-us: Viewed for 90 days from capture date.
    • Non-on-us: Viewed for 30 days from capture date.
  • View Later: On-us, not-on-us, and returned digitized items are available from capture date.
    • A digital item is available but cannot be viewed immediately.
    • The item may be ordered for systematic delivery.
    • Up to 15 items can be selected for View Later.
  • View Now or View Later not available: A digital item may not be available.
    1. Locate the item in RE and press [F11].
    2. Review the IMG column for the code:
  • Digital (D): The item is retrievable through View Now or View Later. If the item is a deposit, click the View link under the Transaction column in OIB to view or order the entire transaction.
  • Microfilm (M): The item can only be requested by submitting a CCN case. Go to step 3 of the View the status of a request section to submit a CCN case based on the transaction type.
  • Both (B): The item may be requested through View now or View Later.
  • No (N): The item may be available by clicking View in the transaction column.

Use the Date Search with the ISN/sequence number. If the item does not appear, no image is available. This procedure is complete.

Branch Resource – DDA Foundational:

Refer to the table below for troubleshooting common issues:

Issue

Details

Blocked image

  • A blocked image is an OIB transaction image that is unavailable for one of the following reasons:
    • Poor quality
    • Mismatch (MICR data does not match the image)
    • Missing image (user receives an image unavailable message)
  • Blocked images display with:
    • A red background in the View Now and View Later columns
    • The message Checkboxes in red indicate possible bad, missing or erroneous images. Please validate account information before providing any copies to the customer in red at the top of the page
  • Only Operations Processors and BC BPSRs may access blocked images.
  • If an image of a blocked item is requested, a BC BPSR must:
    1. Validate that the item belongs to the requestor.
    2. Submit the photocopy order for the banker through OIB as normal.

Content does not load (Please wait error message)

Refer to Adobe > Issues and troubleshooting > Content does not load.

Incorrect items received

Instruct the banker to MAC the incorrect items to Image and Photo Services at S4003-010 and reorder the correct items.

Locating item

If unable to locate an image, use an alternate search option:

  • ISN: An ISN (sequence number) search is the fastest way to locate an item.
    • When using RE to locate an item, press [F11] to obtain the ISN number.
    • On the STMT screen, the ISN is the last 12 digits of the Trace ID number on the 2nd line of the transaction.
    • In OIB, enter the ISN number in the Sequence Number field.
  • Not-on-us account number: If the transaction involves a not-on-us account number:
    • Use the Account Search tab in OIB.
    • Enter the not-on-us account number in the Account field.
  • Routing number (RTN): If the RTN is provided or found in a previous search:
    • Use RE and search by the RTN and transaction amount to locate the ISN.
    • The item may have a different ISN than was found in the original search.

Locating statement

  • Combined statement: View the DDMU.11 screen to determine if the account is part of a combined statement.

If it is, search by the master account number.

  • Lost/stolen transfer: View the DDMU.18 screen to determine if a Lost/Stolen Transfer was completed on the account.

If it was, search by the old account number.

  • Prime or Premier Checking: Access DDMU.28 and review the PMA Relationship Data section to determine if the account is in a Prime or Premier Checking Relationship.

If the account is in a Prime or Premier Checking Relationship, search by the relationship number.

  • Product change: View the Prior Subproduct and Date S/P Transfer fields on DDMU.89 to determine if the account was recently converted to or from a Prime or Premier Checking.

Other issue

When system updates are made to OIB, settings must be reset and additional steps are needed to clear the Edge browser cache.

Example: You are unable to access a statement due to OIB requiring additional fields. 

  1. Open an Edge session.
  2. Select the ellipses (...) at the top right > Settings > Privacy, search and services > Choose what to clear under Clear browsing data.
  3. Select All time under the Time range.
  4. Check the boxes for Browsing history, Download history, Cookies and all other site data, and Cached images and files, if not already checked.
  5. Select Clear now > the Question Mark (?) from the taskbar at the bottom of your computer > WFDC Help Center.
  6. Select Utilities on the left panel > Clear Cache and Cookies > Clear Cache-Edge.
  7. Close all open Edge browser sessions and then relaunch.
