Approvals and Overrides – Teller  - PR2321

 

Published January 20, 2026

 

Summary

 

Request or provide a teller transaction approval or process an assisted approval, self-approval, teller override or self-override on TellerView.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

Understand the rules

 

 

  •       If your Employee ID was used to approve a transaction that you did not authorize, refer to the Fraud Claims and Identity Theft: Unusual Transactions or Account Activity, Elder and Vulnerable Adult Financial Exploitation procedure.
    •      Notify Branch Management
    •      Report suspected employee misconduct through the Cayman Bank EthicsLine Web Reporting System as soon as possible, but no later than the end of the same calendar day the activity or behavior is discovered
  •       When a transaction review or approval is required:
    •      Tell the customer when additional time or assistance is required to review the transaction.
    •      Transaction approvers: Introduce yourself to the customer and explain your role when you are asked to review a transaction or request.
    •      Before attempting to approve or obtain approval for a transaction, always complete a review of all transaction items, all system warning messages, and the Risk Evaluation Tool, when required.

      Note: System messages for the transaction may be reviewed by selecting Go Back on the Override screen.
  •       Transaction approval or reference codes obtained from another department (for example, Treasury Management Client Services [TMCS], The Private Bank [TPB]) must be documented in the Comments field on the Approval screen (for example, TPB code AB12345).

Transactional exceptions

 

 

  •       When a transactional exception is being approved (for example, waiving the identification requirement for a transaction), the exception must be documented on the Approval screen in the Comments field.

When the Approval screen is not available, document the transaction exception on the item and scan or re-scan into TellerView to capture the approval.

  •       Approvers must not make exceptions to transactions they are processing (no self-approvals are permitted).
  •       Only branch management or regional employees may approve transactional exceptions.
  •       Transactional exceptions cannot be made for Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) regulatory procedure requirements. These requirements ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
  •       Item cashing transactions only: Select the Other approval reason option when approving a transactional exception.

 

 

Remote approvals

 

 

  •       When a remote approval is required (transaction exceeds the transaction authority of all employees currently in the branch), call the remote approver to ensure they are available to review and approve the transaction. This will also allow the remote approver to obtain required transaction information.
  •       Remote approvers will receive an approval request notification email and are required to take the following action based on email access at the time of the transaction:
    Reference: TellerView Remote Approval Reference Guide

Note: If a delay occurs in receiving the approval email, use the No email access process to complete the transaction for the best customer experience.

  •      Email access:
    •      Transaction approved: Complete one of the following actions:
      •      Select the I approve link
      •      Verbally provide the 6-digit authorization code.

        Note: If there is more than a 60 second delay in the systematic approval of the transaction using the I approve link, ask the teller to select the Approver does not have access to email checkbox and follow the No email access process.
    •      Transaction declined: Verbally decline the transaction and select I do not attest.
  •      No email access at the time of the transaction:
    •      Transaction approved: Verbally approve the transaction. Complete the notification email approval attestation by selecting I Attest before the end of the same business day.
    •      Transaction not approved: Verbally decline the transaction. Complete the notification email decline decision attestation by selecting I do not attest before the end of the same business day.

Notes:

  •       The remote approver will receive a confirmation of their selected decision (I approve, I attest, or I do not attest).
  •       A BSP Alert will be generated when the transaction approval attestation is not completed by the end of the day the transaction was approved and processed or when you select the I do not attest link but the transaction was processed using your credentials.
  •       If your Employee ID was used to remote approve a transaction that you did not authorize, refer to the Fraud Claims and Identity Theft: Unusual Transactions or Account Activity, Elder and Vulnerable Adult Financial Exploitation procedure:
  •       Notify Branch Management
  •       Report suspected employee misconduct through the Cayman Bank EthicsLine Web Reporting System as soon as possible, but no later than the end of the same calendar day the activity or behavior is discovered.

 

 

Non-Branch Banking employee approvals Fraud Awareness and Loss Prevention

 

 

Complete the following steps when a non-Branch Banking employee (for example, TPB) provides a transaction approval or reference code for a deposit, item cashing, purchase bank check, or debit memo post transaction.

Two separate approvals are required for transactions requiring an approval or reference code from another line of business (for example, TPB, TMCS):

  •       Other line of business approval or reference code: Documented in the Comments field on the Approval screen.
  •       Transaction approval: From a Branch Banking employee (listed on the Approver menu or selected from the Search for Approver field on the Approval screen). 

    Notes: 
    •       TellerView will only allow Branch Banking employees with the appropriate transaction approval authority on Banker Profile to approve deposit, item cashing, purchase bank check, branch settlement, and debit memo post transactions. 
    •       Do not attempt to use the employee from the other line of business (for example TPB, TMCS) as the approver on TellerView.
  1. Obtain an approval or reference code by calling the proper department or other line of business (for example TPB, TMCS).
  2. Document the code in the Comments field on the Approval screen (for example, TPB Approval code AB12345).

    Item cashing transaction only: Select Line of Business Approval Code approval reason option when an approval or reference code was provided by another line of business.
     
  3. Obtain a transaction approval from a Branch Banking employee with the appropriate transaction approval authority in Banker Profile, as outlined within this procedure.

    For transactions above the branch employees’ approval limits, obtain approval and an employee ID from a district manager, regional services consultant, or above with the appropriate transaction approval authority in Banker Profile.

    Note: Do not enter the other line of business employee’s information (for example, the Private Bank Service Team agent) in the Search for Approver field.

  1. If you are willing to accept full transaction responsibility and the transaction amount is within your authority limit, go to the next step.

  • Approved – approver is not present: Go to step 2.

  2. Select the appropriate override reason:

Override reason

Override reason description

1 – Approval given by (approver's name and/or ID)

An approver with the appropriate authority limit provided verbal approval; however, the approver is not physically present or cannot type their User ID and password on the Approval screen.
Comments are required to document the following:

  • The approver's first and last name (do not use User ID, Employee ID, initials, or nicknames).
  • Any approval codes obtained from other departments (for example, Treasury Management Client Services [TMCS], The Private Bank [TPB]).

3 – Positive Pay Payee Validation

  • All warning messages are reviewed.

Either of the following:

  • The check presented is on the list of approved Check Cashing Agreements.

    • Do not use this option if the approving employee is not present. See Override reason, 1 - Approval given by (approver's name and/or ID).

    • All system warning messages are reviewed.
    • Risk Evaluation review is completed.
    • Comments are required to document reasons for the self-override and any approval codes from other departments (for example, TMCS, TPB).

Item cashing approval reasons

 

 

When completing an assisted or self-approval for item cashing transactions, all applicable transaction-related risk-based decision reasons must be selected from the menu on the Approval screen.

References:

  •       Transaction approval guide
  •       Prevent losses with a risk evaluation
  •       Transactional Safeguards and Exceptions
  •       Talking points: Declining over-the-counter transactions, new accounts, and wire transfers

Reason

Description

Account History

No Stops signs are present when account details reviewed. Use the  Risk Evaluation Account Detail section on TellerView, when possible. Account details should include:

  •       Account opened more than 12 months
  •       No overdrafts in last 12 months
  •       Average 12-month balance is greater than transaction amount
  •       No holds or pledges
  •       No return deposit or cashed items

Customer Relationship

The customer record indicates the customer has an extensive relationship with the bank (for example, Affluent, The Private Bank [TPB], High Value).

Note: Ensure the customer has been properly identified and authenticated to reduce the risk of customer impersonation fraud.

Item Review

No stop signs are present when completing an item review.

Note: Even if you are positive of the customer’s identity, review the transaction items to ensure the individual is the intended payee, the items meet negotiability requirements, and have proper endorsements.

  •       Pre-printed checks/items: No signs of counterfeit or alteration
  •       Counter withdrawal (DDA/SAV/Line of credit advance): No signs of customer impersonation

Reference: Detecting withdrawal fraud


Actions should include:

  •       Review companion items in OIB.

    Reference:Operations Image Browser (OIB) User Guide
  •       Items $5,000 or greater:Verify issuance or funds with maker or drawee bank as required when completing a risk evaluation.

    Reference: Transaction Fraud Support (TFS) Quick Reference Guide
  •       Verify security features using resources located on the Fraud Awareness and Loss Prevention website > General Resources > Security Features.
  •       Endorsement is substantially similar to the payee name and account title.

Line of Business Approval Code

Either of the following apply:

Note: The code received from the other line of business must be documented on the Approval screen Comments field (for example, Approval code 12345 – TPB, Reference code 98765 - TFS).

  •       Other line of business: The account is owned by another line of business (for example, TMCS or TPB) and an approval or reference code was obtained for this transaction.
  •       Transaction Fraud Support (TFS): The validity of the transaction has verified by TFS, and they provided a reference number.

Note: Do not select the Line of Business Approval Code reason when TFS cannot verify the validity of the item.

References:

  •      Fraud Claims and Identity Theft: Unusual Transactions or Account Activity, Elder and Vulnerable Adult Financial Exploitation procedure
  •      Transaction Fraud Support (TFS) Quick Reference Guide

Signature

A verification of the maker signature, presenter’s endorsement, or both as applicable (using signatures on file when possible) was completed.

Transaction History

No Stops signs are present when reviewing the Customer/Transaction history section of the Risk Evaluation screen. History details include:

  •       No recent unusual activity
  •       No same day transactions
  •       Account is not or was not recently inactive (last 90 days)
  •       Multiple ATM deposits have not been made
  •       Transaction follows the customer’s typical history (not out of-pattern)
  •       No changes on profile in the past 90 days on the Customer Event History (CEH) screen (for example, phone number, address change)

Known Customer

Do not use. Authentication is documented on the Identification screen.

Other

Branch management or above has approved a transactional exception. Document a complete description of the reason used, including the approver, in the Comments field on the Approval screen (for example, John Manager waived the identification requirement).

  •       Transactional exceptions cannot be made for Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) regulatory procedure requirements. These requirements ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
  •       Only branch management or regional employees may approve transactional exceptions.
  •       Approvers must not make exceptions to transactions they are processing (no self-approvals are permitted).
  •       When additional description is needed and does not fit in the Comments field:
    1.        Document a short description of the transactional exception in the Comments field.
    2.       Completely document the transaction exception on the actual item.
    3.       Scan or re-scan the item into TellerView to capture the written comments.
  •       Inappropriate approval comments should not be used. This includes, but is not limited to, over teller limit, within approved limits, or approved.

 

 

Add a transaction approver to the menu

 

 

For deposit, item cashing, purchase bank check, branch settlement, and debit memo post transactions only.

Branch Operations Associate Manager, Branch Manager (or delegate) or above: Complete the following to add a transaction approver who works at multiple locations to the Select Approver menu on TellerView.

Note: Unless added to the additional AU, a transaction approver’s name will not automatically display in the Select Approver menu at any AU other than their home branch.

  1. Access Banker Profile.
  2. Select the employee’s profile > Location tab > New.
  3. Select the following:
    • Location Category: TRANSACTION
    • Location Type: TRANSACTION APPROVER
  4. Select Add.
  5. Enter the branch AU and select Search.
  6. Select the appropriate branch AU > Add.

Notes:

  • Repeat step 4-6 to add another AU.
  • The Select Approver menu will display the newly added transaction approver within two business days.
