Payment Maintenance: Consumer and Small Business Lending - PR472

 

Published December 4, 2025

 

Summary

 

Handle customer requests related to payment maintenance for consumer and small business lending accounts. Assist with payoff and paydown quotes, deferrals, extensions, re-ages, rewrites, and changes to payment options or amounts.

 

 

Understand the rules

 

 

  • The customer must have authority to request this transaction.
  • Do not:
    • Give printed SVP, Hogan, or Shaw screens with payment, payoff, or transaction information to customers.
    • Process incoming phone requests to move funds.
  • Line of credit, credit card, and loan account payments received in the branches will receive credit for the payment effective on the day the customer made the payment.
  • A business cannot request or require an active duty servicemember to use a military allotment to repay an obligation secured by tangible and moveable personal property.
  • All new personal loans accounts receive billing statements rather than coupon books.
  • Accounts that qualify for non-monthly payments receive a billing statement according to the frequency of the payment schedule.
  • Refer to the Unusual Activity Referral (UAR) Requirements procedure when you suspect unusual activity.
  • If the customer alleges a Cayman Bank employee opened the account without their consent (unauthorized), or the account features or terms do not align with details discussed with the Cayman Bank employee at account opening (unwanted):
    • Immediately contact the appropriate department using the banker-facing phone numbers listed below to review options for the account closure. Tell the processing department the closure reason is due to the account being unauthorized or unwanted and related to sales practices allegations.
    • File an escalated complaint and document the following:
      • Complainant (customer) asserts a potential allegation of sales practice misconduct or unethical behavior related to a potentially unauthorized or unwanted account
      • Account number
      • Name or description of the unwanted or unauthorized product or service
      • The customer’s request

Examples: Close the account, leave it open, no preference

      • Do not request or demand payment.
      • The customer may be informed any past due payments must be made in order for the due date change to occur. This procedure is complete.

  • Their next payment amount may increase after changing the due date if the number of days in the billing cycle also increases.
