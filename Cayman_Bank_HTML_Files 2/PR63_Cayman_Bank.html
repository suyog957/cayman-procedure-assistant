Coin-Counting Machines: Sell Full Coin Bags, Balance, Test - PR63

 

Published October 9, 2025

 

Summary

 

Branches with an approved coin-counting machine only: Sell full bags of coin from the coin-counting machine to the branch vault, perform a weekly balance or monthly random cash count on the coin-counting machine in dual control, and complete monthly testing.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

 

Understand the rules

 

 

  • Coin-counting machines must be:
    • Used for U.S. coin only.
    • Located in a secure area.
    • Maintained, accessed, and locked in dual control.
    • Locked at all times.

Exception: Branches with written approval from the Branch Manager may leave the coin-counting machine unlocked during business hours. A copy of the completed and approved Coin Counting Machine Exception Approval (596391) form must be maintained in the branch Security File.

Reference:Records Management > Retention Schedule

  • Complete coin machines tasks using the appropriate level of control:
    • Weekly balancing and random monthly cash counts: Dual control
    • Monthly coin-counting machine testing: Single control
  • Adhere to all dual control requirements in the Dual Control and Single Control procedure when completing dual control tasks associated with maintaining, accessing, and balancing the coin counting machine.
  • Employees must have appropriate authorities in Banker Profile:
    • Vault Access authority to sign on to the vault cashline (00 or 50)
    • Dual Control authority to be part of a dual control team
  • Sell full coin bags from coin-counting machines to the branch vault cashline (00 or 50).
  • Complete all steps to sell coin bags to the branch vault consecutively, including applicable TellerView transactions, while maintaining dual control.
  • Rotate the following tasks according to the applicable rotation frequency requirements in the Dual Control and Single Control procedure:
    • Weekly coin-counting machine balancing
    • Monthly random cash counts of the coin-counting machine
    • Monthly testing of the coin-counting machine

Note: Refer to the Random Cash Counts Dashboard on the Bank Supervision – Reporting site on Branch Portal to view previous dual control teams or TellerView T657 View All Cash Lines to see the last two dual control teams who completed random cash counts on the coin-counting machine cashline.

  • The maximum amount of coin that can be stored overnight in a single coin-counting machine is $5,250.
  • Assign user IDs, keys, and combinations on Security Control.

Related procedure: Security Control

  • Refer to your equipment procedures for processing loose coin through the coin machine. See your manager if you are unsure of these steps.

 

 

 

Sell coins to Branch Vault

 

 

  1. In dual control, remove each full bag from the coin-counting machine and ensure it is:
    • Sealed.
    • Kept out of customer view.
    • Placed in a locked unit or compartment in the branch vault as soon as possible.
  2. Place an empty coin bag on the coin machine to replace the full bag.
  3. Sign on to the applicable cashline in dual control and select T611 Buy Sell Cash to sell all coin bags removed to the vault cashline (00 or 50).
    1. Select the compartment where the coin will be stored.
    2. Complete the Cash Detail screen, by denomination (not a lump sum), for the compartment selected.
    3. Select Add Compartment and repeat steps a and b if coin bags will be stored in multiple compartments.
    4. Select Submit.
    5. Enter dual control credentials.
  4. Secure each sealed coin bag in the branch vault compartment selected in the T611 transaction until ready for shipment.

Related procedure:Outgoing Currency and Coin Shipments

 

 

 

Weekly balance

 

 

Balance all coin-counting machines in dual control at least weekly. If an employee completes a buy/sell transaction after balancing and before Perm Off, rebalance the cashline on TellerView.

  1. Ensure all employees complete their buy/sell transactions on TellerView before balancing.
  2. Obtain the balancing information from the coin-counting machine in dual control.
  3. Sign on to the appropriate coin-counting machine cashline on TellerView in dual control.
  4. Select T614 Balancing.
  5. Enter each coin denomination amount. Do not enter a lump sum.
  6. Select Daily in the Counting For drop-down menu and enter the balancing information.
  7. Determine if the cashline balances:
    • Balances or has a difference of $3 or less: Select Ok.
    • Does not balance: TellerView automatically navigates to the cash difference transaction. Follow the steps in the Balancing: Drop and Go, Teller, and Vault > Process a cash difference on TellerView procedure.
  8. Complete a Perm Off when balancing is complete.

 

 

 

Random monthly cash count

 

 

Follow requirements for random monthly cash counts in the Cash Counts and Random Monthly Cash Detail > Unannounced random monthly cash count procedure.

  1. Obtain the balancing information from the machine in dual control.
  2. Sign on to the appropriate coin-counting machine cashline on TellerView in dual control.
  3. Select T614 Balancing.
  4. Enter each coin denomination amount (not a lump sum).
  5. Select Shared in the Counting For drop-down menu and enter the balancing information.
  6. Select Random Monthly in the Reason for Count drop-down menu.
  7. Determine if the cashline balances:
    • Balances or has a difference $3 or less: Go to the next step.
    • Does not balance: If the difference cannot be resolved:
      1. Complete the File Out of Balance screen to process the difference.
      2. In the Comments field, provide details to explain the difference, if known, using acronyms in the Balancing: Drop and Go, Teller, and Vault > Process a cash difference procedure.
  8. Follow the system prompts to enter dual control credentials and submit the balance.
  9. Complete a Perm Off when complete.
