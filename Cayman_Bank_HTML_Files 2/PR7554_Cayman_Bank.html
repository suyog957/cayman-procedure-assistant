Incoming Currency and Coin Shipments - PR7554

 

Published January 30, 2026

 

Summary

 

Receive and verify incoming currency and coin shipments.

 

Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

 

Dual control requirements

 

 

Complete activities associated with receiving, verifying, and securing incoming cash shipments in dual control in a secure area.

  • Employees responsible for cash shipments must have Dual Control authority on their Authority Record.
  • One of the employees must also have Vault Access authority.
  • Complete all steps for receiving and verifying a shipment while maintaining dual control, including associated TellerView transactions.
  • Counting incoming shipment cash, including bundle and strap counting currency, may be done by one employee with the second employee observing. If a discrepancy is identified, the second employee must count the cash while the first employee observes, to confirm if the first employee’s count is accurate.
  • If your branch does not have a desktop workstation inside the vault to process the shipment on TellerView:
    • Secure and lock all cash in a dual-control compartment before leaving the vault to process TellerView transactions.
    • TellerView transactions can be completed at the same time, separate from the physical transfer of cash to vault compartments where the shipment will be stored.

 

Contact armored transportation

 

 

Email armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com if there are issues with the armored courier. In the email, include:

  • Your branch AU
  • Date of the delivery
  • What you were expecting in the shipment, for example the amount of currency and coin by denomination
  • A summary of the issue:
    • Courier did not arrive on the scheduled day
    • Courier left before releasing an incoming shipment
    • Courier did not have identification
    • Courier refused to provide identification
    • Courier refused to wait while the shipment was reviewed for signs of tampering and currency was bundle or strap counted through the bags
    • Missing bag of currency or box of coin
    • Extra box of coin or bag of currency delivered in error the courier refused to take back
    • Bag of currency delivered in error, for example wrong AU or bag previously shipped out by your branch and returned in error
    • Tampered bag of currency or box of coin
  • Request for a ticket number

Note: Failure to email armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com as soon as possible could result in:

  • A loss to your branch when there is a missing or extra bag of currency or box of coin.
  • Your branch waiting for cash, when there is a missed delivery or the courier left without delivering a shipment.

 

Allow courier access to secure area

 

 

  • The armored courier must be identified each time they visit, even if it is the same courier week after week. 
  • Do not require an armored courier to wait in a customer line or lobby. Identify them and escort them to a secure area as soon as possible.
  • Allowing a courier access to a secure area may be done in single control.
  1. Review the armored courier’s identification cards and identify them according to the vendor’s identification policy each time they visit.

    If the armored courier does not have identification or will not provide identification, do not allow them access to a secure area and do not accept the incoming shipment. Email armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com to report the armored courier according to instructions in the Contact armored transportation section.
    • Brinks: Review both the identification card and signature card.
    • Garda: Review both the front and back of the authorized agent card.
    • Loomis: Review both the identification card and signature card.
    • Axiom: Review both the identification card and signature card.
    • Granite City, LL&D (Respond), Miracle, or Rochester: If needed, email armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com to request these vendors’ identification policies. Include your branch AU in the email.
  2. Escort the identified armored courier to a secure area where you will process the shipment exchange in dual control.
  3. Go to the Receive shipment from courier section to inspect the bags of currency and boxes of coin and sign for the shipment
     

 

Receive shipment from courier

 

 

  • Complete steps in this section in dual control in a secure area.
  • If a T801 Incoming Shipment > Create Shipment was created in error, do not Receive or Reject the shipment in TellerView. It will automatically be removed from the T801 screen after approximately one week.
  • Follow the Separation of dual-control vault cash procedure to ensure you separate cash appropriately.
  • If the courier must make multiple trips into the branch with an incoming shipment, until the courier has brought in all cash, remain in dual control in a secure area with the first shipment bags or boxes of coin. Only begin inspecting the bags of currency and boxes of coin in the presence of the courier. Only sign for cash you’ve received and inspected.
  • If the courier delivers customer deposit bags with an incoming branch cash shipment, ensure the branch cash shipment is processed and secured before completing the T682 Courier Delivered Log transaction.
  1. Inspect the bags of currency and boxes of coin before the courier leaves the branch.

     
    1. Confirm the bags of currency belong to your AU.
      • Reject and do not accept extra bags of currency or boxes of coin delivered in error.
      • If the courier refuses to take extra boxes of coin or bags of currency delivered in error, do not reject the entire shipment. Accept the extra bag of currency or box of coin and process it according to this procedure, separate from other boxes of coin or bags of currency received. Do not open or use the extra bag of currency or box of coin delivered in error.
    2. Carefully inspect all sides of the shipping bags for signs of tampering.
      • Pull at the seams of the bag, looking for tears or cuts.
      • Look for clear tape on the bag or small holes.
      • Confirm the bag is sealed correctly.

Reject and do not accept bags of currency or boxes of coin with evidence of tampering.
Note: Failure to identify a tampered bag may result in a loss to your branch. 

  1. Bundle or strap-count the currency through the bag to calculate a bag total and count boxed coin.
  2. Sign the courier’s PDA device for the amount of cash accepted from the courier. If your courier does not have a PDA device, they will provide a paper manifest for you to sign.
    • If the amount accepted is different than the PDA device or manifest, ask the courier to update the PDA device or manifest or provide a new paper manifest for the amount received. If the courier cannot update their PDA and doesn’t have a paper manifest, refuse the shipment and contact Armored Transportation.
    • Maintain possession of shipments in dual control after the shipment has been reviewed, signed for, and taken from the armored courier.
    • Do not:
      • Allow the armored courier to help move the incoming shipment bags after you have taken possession of the bags.
      • Request or allow the courier to place the currency or coin in a vault, safe, or cash compartment.
  1. If a missing bag of currency or box of coin is arriving after the expected delivery date go to the Process partial shipment – missing portion from original shipment now arrived section.
  2. Review the Cash in Transit (CIT) ticket received with the shipment.

If any of the following apply, immediately go to the Missing or incorrect Cash in Transit ticket section and complete the applicable action, then return to step 4 in this section:

  • Montana or Wyoming branches that order from the Federal Reserve
  • No CIT or GL ticket was received with the shipment
  • Missing box of coin or bag of currency
  • Extra box of coin or bag of currency delivered in error the courier refused to take back
  • Cash discrepancy (for example, a missing bundle or strap of currency)

    Note: If the CIT ticket matches the amount of cash received but does not match the amount ordered, this is not a cash discrepancy. This may happen when a cash vault has limited coin and cannot fulfill the order for coin requested.
  1. Sign on to the vault cashline (00 or 50) and select T801 Incoming Shipment.
  2. Locate the order for the incoming shipment by order number or amount.
  • An order will not automatically display for:
    • Touch-Tone Servicing orders
    • MT/WY Federal Reserve orders
    • Extra bags of currency or boxes of coin received in error the courier refused to take

In these cases, complete T801 Incoming Shipment > Create Shipment.

  • Enter 10-digits of one number in the Order Number field (for example, 1111111111) for the following situations:
    • Branches regularly receiving an order in two deliveries (for example, coin separate from currency).
    • Extra bags of currency or boxes of coin received in error the courier refused to take.
  1. Compare the order amount on the screen to the amount received. 
  • Amounts match: Select Receive.
  • Amounts do not match: 
    • Select Reject.
    • Select a reason for rejecting the shipment.
    • Enter dual control credentials.
    • Complete T801 Incoming Shipment > Create Shipment for the amount of cash received, including a denomination breakdown. Then repeat steps 5 and 6 for the shipment created.
  1. Scan the CIT ticket and complete required fields.
  2. Select the compartment where the unverified shipment will be stored and select Submit.

    Note: Do not store incoming shipment bags of currency in a coin cage.
     
  3. Select the Cash Detail Received checkboxes to confirm the amount of each denomination received. 
    Note: A shipment will be rejected on the T801 Cash Detail screen when a Received checkbox is not selected for a denomination.
     
  4. Select Complete.
  5. Enter dual control credentials.
  6. Select either verify now or verify later:
  • Verify Now: TellerView will auto-navigate to the T802 Verify/Break Down Shipment transaction. Go to the Verify the shipment section.
  • Verify Later: 
    1. Complete the T801 transaction.

      If the shipment bag was opened to remove the CIT ticket (the ticket was not in an external pouch):
      1. Write the names of the dual control employees who completed the T801 transaction on the outside of a new tamper-evident bag.
      2. Seal the new tamper-evident bag before securing in dual control.

        The same dual control employees who opened the shipment bag to remove the CIT ticket must verify the shipment and complete the T802 Verify/Break Down Shipment transaction. 
         
    2. Secure the unverified shipment in the dual control compartment selected in the T801 transaction.
    3. Complete the steps in the Verify the shipment section by the end of the day.
  1. Complete the following if any of these situations apply:
  • Missing bag of currency or box of coin
  • Bag of currency or box of coin rejected due to evidence of tampering
  • Bag of currency or box of coin delivered in error (extra currency or coin not ordered, wrong AU, or previously shipped out by your branch)

    Note: These steps may be done in single control.
  1. Email armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com immediately to report the shipment issue according to instructions in the Contact armored transportation section. 
  2. Notify the branch manager or branch lead designee and provide: 
    • Proof of loss or damage, only for bags or boxes with evidence of tampering
    • Shipment documentation and description of the shipment error
    • Expected delivery date for missing bag of currency or box of coin
    • Expected pick up date for extra bag or currency or box of coin
    • Total value of the expected shipment

 

Verify the shipment

 

 

  • Complete all steps in this section in dual control in a secure area.
  • Verify the shipment in dual control on the same day it is delivered.
    • Cayman Bank cash vault: Report discrepancies by the end of the same day the shipment is received.
    • Outsourced cash vault (Loomis or Garda is printed on the Cash In Transit ticket received): Report discrepancies no later than 2:00 p.m. Mountain Time the following business day.
    • Federal Reserve: Report discrepancies immediately after identifying them.
  • If the shipment bag was opened to remove the Cash in Transit (CIT) ticket, the same dual control employees who opened the shipment bag to remove the CIT ticket must complete the T802 Verify/Break Down Shipment transaction.
  • Branches servicing ATMs: Move ATM cash listed on the iCom Order Details into TellerView ATM ID compartments on your incoming shipment day. Failure to do so will negatively impact future cash ordering forecasts for both branch and ATM cash.
  • Follow the Separation of dual-control vault cash procedure to ensure you separate cash appropriately. 
  1. Open the shipment bags and remove the currency.

    Exception: Do not open or use extra bags of currency or boxes of coin delivered in error. Bundle and strap count the currency through the bag to complete the verification process.
     
  2. Count the currency (bundle or strap count) and boxed coin. Consider documenting the amounts by denomination for entry in TellerView.
  3. Complete the following if you identify:
  • Loose currency because a strap broke
  • Currency straps stamped by another Cayman Bank branch
  • Blank currency straps

 

Note: These steps do not apply to currency straps with a cash vault date stamp, a Federal Reserve date stamp, or Bureau of Engraving and Printing (BEP) printed on them.

  1. Detail-count and re-strap the currency.
  2. Write or stamp the following information on each strap: 
    • Branch name and AU
    • Date shipment was received and verified
    • Initials or signature of the dual-control employees completing the detail-count

Refer to the Process Suspected Counterfeit Bills, Unfit, Mutilated, or Contaminated Currency and Coin procedure if suspected counterfeit currency is found when detail counting currency.

  1. If your branch does not have a desktop workstation in the vault area, secure the currency and coin in a dual control compartment before leaving the vault to process the T802 Verify / Break Down Shipment transaction.
  2. Sign on to the vault cashline (00 or 50).
  3. Select T802 Verify/Break Down Shipment if you did not auto-navigate from the T801 Incoming Shipment transaction.
  4. Select the incoming shipment to verify.
  5. Complete the Cash Detail Verified Amounts fields by denomination (not a lump sum) for the counted amounts.
  6. If the Verified amounts do not match the Amounts listed in shipment: 
    1. Select No in the popup message.
    2. Recount the cash by denomination and validate entries are correct.

      Note: TellerView compares the Amounts listed in for shipment to the amounts you enter in the Verified amounts fields by individual denomination. Even if the Total amounts match, the popup message will display if individual denominations do not match.
    3. If amounts do not match after a recount, select Yes in the popup message. 

      Note: If the Total amounts do not match, selecting Yes will adjust the compartment amount only. The vault cashline will be out of balance by the difference.
       
  7. Select the first compartment where cash will be stored and complete the Cash Detail by denomination (not a lump sum) for the cash to be stored there.
  8. If cash will be stored in additional compartments, select Add Compartment and repeat step 8.
  9. Select Submit when all currency and coin has been moved to the appropriate compartments and there are no Remaining Funds $ to move.
  10. Enter dual control credentials.
  11. Branches servicing ATMs: Complete a T805 Compartment Transfer transaction to transfer the iCom Order Details ATM Requirement amounts to the TellerView ATM ID compartment for the designated ATM.
  • This step is not required if ATM cash was moved to TellerView ATM compartments when completing the T802 transaction.
  • Only transfer ATM cash amounts to TellerView ATM compartments. Do not transfer branch cash amounts to TellerView ATM compartments, unless instructed by ATM Cash Optimization when ATM cash is running low.
  • Complete the Add Cash to the ATM section of the ATM: Servicing procedure if you are adding cash to the ATM at this time.
  1. If you have not already done so, secure the currency and coin in the compartments selected in the T802 and T805 transactions.
  2. Retain the following according to the Records Management > Retention Schedule, if applicable. This step may be done in single control.
    • iCom Order Details, T611 Print Cash Detail, or Cash Vault Order Form printed when the order was placed
    • Shipment packing slips
    • Courier paper manifests
  3. Cash discrepancy identified when receiving or verifying the shipment:

    For example: the shipment is short a bundle or strap of currency. 

    Note: Steps a and b may be done in single control. 
    1. Locate the Cash In Transit (CIT) or GL ticket scanned in the T801 transaction.
    2. Notify the branch manager or branch lead designee of the cash discrepancy. Provide shipment documentation and a description of the discrepancy.
    3. Retain shipment bags until the currency discrepancy is resolved.
    4. Refer to the applicable section to process the cash discrepancy.
      • Process cash discrepancies: Cayman Bank or outsourced cash vault
      • Process cash discrepancies: Federal Reserve Bank
  4. If an extra bag of currency or box of coin was delivered in error or a bag of currency or box of coin is missing, ensure an email was sent to armoredtransportation@Cayman Bank.com as outlined in the Contact armored transportation section.
    • For extra bags of currency or boxes of coin received in error:
      • The Armored Transportation Resource Center will arrange for a courier to pick up the extra currency or coin.
      • Do not open or use the currency or coin. Keep it separate from other branch cash until it is picked up by the armored courier.
    • On the day the courier will arrive to pick up the extra currency or coin, follow the Outgoing Currency and Coin Shipments procedure to complete the:
      • T803 Prepare Outgoing Shipment transaction
      • General ledger ticket for the extra currency or coin amount
      • T804 Release Outgoing Shipment transaction

Process cash discrepancies: Cayman Bank or outsourced cash vault

 

 

Resolve shipment cash discrepancies with a Cayman Bank or outsourced cash vault when the amount of cash received is different than the Cash In Transit (CIT) ticket. For example, the shipment bag is short a bundle or strap of currency.

Retain shipment bags until currency discrepancies are resolved.

Do not contact the cash vault to resolve the following discrepancies:

  • Missing or extra box of coin or bag of currency: This is resolved by contacting Armored Transportation.
  • CIT ticket matches the amount of cash received but does not match the order: This may happen when a cash vault has limited coin and cannot fulfill the order for coin requested. It is not a shipment cash discrepancy.

The branch vault cashline (00 or 50) must take the cash difference for currency and coin discrepancies identified after your cash vault’s discrepancy reporting deadline.

Go to the applicable sub-section based on the cash vault type: 

Note: Loomis or Garda is printed on the CIT ticket when your branch is serviced by an outsourced cash vault. If the CIT ticket received does not have Loomis or Garda printed on it, refer to the Process cash discrepancies – Cash from Cayman Bank cash vault section.

Process cash discrepancies – Outsourced cash vault

 

 

Discrepancies must be reported to an outsourced cash vault by 2:00 p.m. Mountain Time the following business day after receiving the shipment.

If strapped currency has an overage or shortage, retain the currency strap until the discrepancy is resolved.
