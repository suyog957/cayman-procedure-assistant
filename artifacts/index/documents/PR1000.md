## Summary

[PR1000-4652c837e666]
Notarize physical documents in person, comply with state statutes, update commission info, and adhere to Cayman Bank’s guidelines for customers and non-Cayman Bank customers.

## Notarizing at Cayman Bank locations

[PR1000-96e53f4512fd]
If you need to obtain or renew a notary commission or need help with notary information including notary supplies, refer to Branch Portal > Branch Tools > Notary Information link.
Existing notaries new to Cayman Bank only: Complete the Employee Information for Existing Notaries New to Cayman Bank (597201) form and submit it to the National Notary Association (NNA) following the instructions on the form
An employee must have both a valid commission from their applicable state and current commission information listed on Banker Profile to perform notarizations at Cayman Bank locations.
Display signage (commission or license) if required by your state.

[PR1000-02a06dc19594]
All Cayman Bank branch employee notaries are covered by the corporate insurance program for Error’s and Omission’s (E&O) insurance while performing work in the scope of their job, including notary services.
Locations may limit to 50 notarizations or one hour of service per requester, whichever comes first. If a substantial request disrupts bank operations, an appointment should be scheduled for the remaining documents.

## Understand the rules

[PR1000-4e9bb862875c]
Notary publics are commissioned by the state from which they obtain their notary commission, not Cayman Bank.
Employees commissioned as notaries are required to follow their applicable state statutes when executing notary duties. If there are questions requiring a deeper understanding of applicable laws or regulations, notaries should use the following resources for state-specific questions:Your state notary website from your Secretary of State or state agency National Notary Association (NNA) hotline at 1-888-876-0827
Employees who obtain or renew a notary commission must complete the affiant and notary training course through Develop You every other year.

[PR1000-23829a763c74]
Notaries must adhere to the following requirements when providing notary services:Cayman Bank Customers: Notarize both Cayman Bank documents and non-Cayman Bank documents. Note: A Cayman Bank customer is an individual who has transaction authority on any Cayman Bank account relationship. Non-customers: Notarize only Cayman Bank documents unless the notary’s state laws provide an exception. Exceptions: Notaries in Arizona, Iowa, New York, South Dakota, and Utah must notarize both Cayman Bank documents and non-Cayman Bank documents regardless of their customer status.

Notarize documents only at a Cayman Bank branch during business hours. Do not go offsite to complete notarizations during business work hours to conduct bank business transactions. ## Complete the notary journal entry

[PR1000-3f6bd26838d5]
Cayman Bank requires using a bound notary journal to record all notarizations, even in states where notary statutes do not require a journal.
Only the notary may make changes in the notary journal.
A separate journal entry and customer signature is required for each document notarized.
Shield personal information from previous notarizations using a sheet of paper or similar object when obtaining the signature of the signer in the journal.
Related procedure: Information Security, Classification, and Compromised Data Incidents
Refer to the inside cover of notary journal for a sample on how to complete.

[PR1000-74c1b17a5c6a]
If permitted by your state, you may use a diagonal line (/) or ditto marks (") only when exact information repeats. However, do not use these in the signature or thumbprint sections.
Exception for California notaries: Do not use ditto marks or signatures across two spaces.
Complete the journal entry following your state-specific guidelines. For states not requiring a journal, the following fields are suggested to complete:

## Notary commissions not renewed or are revoked

[PR1000-65e9dc410ebc]
If a notary commission is not renewed or is revoked, the notary must:
Contact their Secretary of State or applicable state agency office, for instructions related to the notary’s supplies (for example, notary stamp, notary journal) and revoking their commission.
Tell their manager of the non-renew or revocation. The manager can then update the notary’s Banker Profile.

## Manager responsibilities

[PR1000-0744bf4b3e32]
Managers of notary employees or their designee must complete the following:
Monthly review:Ensure notaries use a bound notary journal for notarizations. Confirm the notary’s personnel file has required documentation on file (new, renewing, or transfer). Verify the Banker Profile certification tab includes current commission information. Ensure the notary’s personal and business contact information is updated with the state notary agency.

[PR1000-317db0dd92e4]
Banker Profile Certification tab:Add or modify the notary’s commission number and expiration date when a notary's commission is obtained or renewed. Notes:Enter all 9s in the Commission Number field if the notary’s state does not provide one. The hiring manager updates commission details on the Banker Profile Certification tab for roving bankers. Delete the notary’s commission number and expiration date within 90 days of expiration or revocation.

[PR1000-24a017aedd9e]
Personnel File (new, renewal or transfer): Obtain and file a copy of the following documents in the employee’s personnel file:Notary’s commission from the state agency. Signed Cayman Bank Employee Notary Public Agreement. Note: File the documents at the employee’s primary work location if the employee is a roving banker.
Note: If the employee notary is a Roving branch employee, ensure the documents are filed at the employee’s designated home branch.
