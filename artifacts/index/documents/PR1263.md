## Understand the rules

[PR1263-c10107adcc90]
Use secure destruction to protect the confidentiality of bank and customer information.
Shred vendors or secure-destruction services:Branches must use only the approved shred vendor or secure-destruction service selected for their region, or an approved in-location, cross-cut shredder. Management must ensure the approved vendor or service provides locking, secure-destruction bins or similar service containers for securely destroying dry trash. Do not remove documents or items from vendor-provided, secure-destruction bins.
Exception: In urgent situations, submit a Container Access Request to open the bin on Shred Services Order Forms. If dispatched, the vendor may submit additional charges.

[PR1263-151b0f3cc21e]
Secure-destruction bin keys:Retained by Shred Services, not by the branch. Cannot be assigned to, or in the possession of, employees. Do not give to contract employees or janitorial services.
Ensure secure destruction bins are:Not accessible to customers. Always locked and secured or under camera coverage within a Cayman Bank location (not in lobby areas). Chained or locked in the back of the retail location if the branch is without space for the bin. Not used for wet trash disposal (for example, break room and bathroom trash). Not overfilled where items can easily be removed.

[PR1263-77fa79006b3c]
Note: If the bin is full and must be emptied before the scheduled pickup, submit an Off-Schedule Service Request on Shred Services Order Forms.
