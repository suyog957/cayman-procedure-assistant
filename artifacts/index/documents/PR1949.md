## Understand the rules

[PR1949-e36ef35ddbd7]
TellerView typically allows transaction reversals within 1 hour of completion or until the batch is closed for transactions included in a batch, whichever occurs first. After one hour, you cannot return the physical savings bond to the customer because bond processing has begun. Note: When the bond from a reversed transaction is returned to the customer and has been branded, stamped, or signed, the customer must contact Treasure Direct to redeem the bond. Related procedure:Search the Electronic Journal and Reverse Teller Transactions
