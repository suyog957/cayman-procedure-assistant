## Summary

[PR2306-c063523347db]
Obtain a copy of an account statement including year-end statements, debit, credit, or return item posted to an account. Assist with a request for printed account information. Retrieve document images for checking, savings, safe deposit box, and time accounts.
Select the section heading to expand the procedure content.

## Understand the rules

[PR2306-0b15abd64b2f]
The customer must have authority to request this transaction.
A copy of a previously deposited payment slip is only available for legal purposes (for example, an IRS audit).
If the requestor is a non-Prime or Premier Checking account owner requesting a copy of a statement, provide only the pages related to the non-Prime or Premier Checking account owner.
If an owner was removed and the account remains open, the former owner can only request copies for the time period during which they were an owner.

[PR2306-5a4c0bc806b2]
Do not:Fax, email, or mail statement copies directly to a third-party (individuals or entities) without transaction authority on the specified account. Note: One-time requests to fax statements are not permitted Fax the following statement or transaction information to customers:Copies of statements printed from OIB or MARS SVP Checking or Savings Account History screen prints Form 100, 120, 200, 300, 400, and 500 statements Provide copies of customer statements to Cayman Bank Home Mortgage Consultants (HMCs). Refer HMCs to their current process guides for information on obtaining general account information.

[PR2306-23628a36bf87]
Exception: Customers with disabilities may request to have their account statement faxed to them on a regular or recurring basis. Go to the Maintain Statements procedure for additional information and to complete the request.

## Requests for printed account information

[PR2306-33d65a9f533d]
Never encourage or suggest a customer to share online banking credentials with others to access account information.
Do not accept customer requests to email or mail account information directly to third-party individuals or entities without transaction authority on the requested account.
Requests for account information may be verbally communicated to the customer or a printed copy may be provided.
