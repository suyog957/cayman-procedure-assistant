Maintain and certify a customer's TIN, TIN type, or foreign status on Form W-9 or Form W-8BEN, and reverse or refund current year backup federal tax withholding on an interest earning deposit account.

## Understand the rules

[PR607-81cf7117d72f]
Do not: Provide tax advice to a customer. Refer them to their tax advisor or to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for assistance.
Example: Customer asks for a tax status.
Apply for a TIN on behalf of a customer or assist customers with applying for a TIN or provide advice regarding the application process. Refer them to their tax advisor, the IRS, or to their local Social Security Administration (SSA) office for assistance.
The customer must have authority to request this transaction.

[PR607-1efc3eb81ab0]
Only the following individuals have authority to certify a TIN on an account: Consumer account: Primary tax-responsible account owner and individuals authorized to certify on the customer’s behalf (for example, POA, signer on minor-by account, trustee) Business account: An owner, partner, or key executive with transaction authority on the account
If a U.S. citizen, U.S. Permanent Resident, or customer who is considered a U.S. person for tax purposes is a joint owner on an account with a non-U.S. person or non- U.S. entity, the U.S. person or U.S. entity must be the primary tax-responsible owner on the account.

[PR607-d0f68ec5064b]
A TIN may have the same digits as another TIN if the TIN type is different. Example: An employer identification number (EIN) may be the same digits as a Social Security number (SSN).

[PR607-7ca2f3a6d386]
For stateless customer: W-9 generated by SVP does not require additional action. W-8BEN will be created with Stateless listed as the country of citizenship. This is considered invalid by the IRS, which will result in 24% interest withholding. To create or replace the form with a valid form. Access and manually complete a Substitute Form W-8BEN (596034). Ask the customer to review the IRS form completion instructions for country of citizenship and provide a country meeting the criteria. If the customer indicates they are a U.S. citizen or resident alien, complete a Substitute Form W-9 (595925) instead. Scan the manually completed and signed form to replace the invalid form.

[PR607-8ca6a9c5d4dd]
A W-9 form is generated for the following: U.S. citizens. Non-U.S. citizens who satisfy the green card test and have lawful U.S. permanent residency status. They are issued a TIN and a Permanent Resident Card or Resident Alien Card. Non-U.S. citizens who do not have U.S. permanent residency status but satisfy the substantial presence test. Days present while holding a special visa, for example a student or diplomatic visa, are not counted. If the customer is unsure whether they meet the substantial presence test: Refer the customer to their Tax Advisor.

[PR607-b71fbe6e95cc]

This customer will be subject to 24% withholding and form 1099 reporting on interest earned because they cannot provide an SSN or ITIN.
