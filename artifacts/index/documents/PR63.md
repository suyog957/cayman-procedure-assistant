## Summary

[PR63-43a003871115]
Branches with an approved coin-counting machine only: Sell full bags of coin from the coin-counting machine to the branch vault, perform a weekly balance or monthly random cash count on the coin-counting machine in dual control, and complete monthly testing.

## Understand the rules

[PR63-a1dba609ddc4]
Coin-counting machines must be:Used for U.S. coin only. Located in a secure area. Maintained, accessed, and locked in dual control. Locked at all times.
Exception: Branches with written approval from the Branch Manager may leave the coin-counting machine unlocked during business hours. A copy of the completed and approved Coin Counting Machine Exception Approval (596391) form must be maintained in the branch Security File.
Reference:Records Management > Retention Schedule
Complete coin machines tasks using the appropriate level of control:Weekly balancing and random monthly cash counts: Dual control Monthly coin-counting machine testing: Single control

[PR63-99eda28fbdec]
Adhere to all dual control requirements in the Dual Control and Single Control procedure when completing dual control tasks associated with maintaining, accessing, and balancing the coin counting machine.
Employees must have appropriate authorities in Banker Profile:Vault Access authority to sign on to the vault cashline (00 or 50) Dual Control authority to be part of a dual control team
Sell full coin bags from coin-counting machines to the branch vault cashline (00 or 50).
Complete all steps to sell coin bags to the branch vault consecutively, including applicable TellerView transactions, while maintaining dual control.

[PR63-370ffcb6a01d]
Rotate the following tasks according to the applicable rotation frequency requirements in the Dual Control and Single Control procedure:Weekly coin-counting machine balancing Monthly random cash counts of the coin-counting machine Monthly testing of the coin-counting machine
Note: Refer to the Random Cash Counts Dashboard on the Bank Supervision – Reporting site on Branch Portal to view previous dual control teams or TellerView T657 View All Cash Lines to see the last two dual control teams who completed random cash counts on the coin-counting machine cashline.
The maximum amount of coin that can be stored overnight in a single coin-counting machine is $5,250.

Assign user IDs, keys, and combinations on Security Control.

## Sell coins to Branch Vault

[PR63-88c55b9cba0c]
In dual control, remove each full bag from the coin-counting machine and ensure it is:Sealed. Kept out of customer view. Placed in a locked unit or compartment in the branch vault as soon as possible.
Place an empty coin bag on the coin machine to replace the full bag.

[PR63-727ce4f4693a]
Sign on to the applicable cashline in dual control and select T611 Buy Sell Cash to sell all coin bags removed to the vault cashline (00 or 50).Select the compartment where the coin will be stored. Complete the Cash Detail screen, by denomination (not a lump sum), for the compartment selected. Select Add Compartment and repeat steps a and b if coin bags will be stored in multiple compartments. Select Submit. Enter dual control credentials.
Secure each sealed coin bag in the branch vault compartment selected in the T611 transaction until ready for shipment.
Related procedure:Outgoing Currency and Coin Shipments

## Weekly balance

[PR63-5a5c152bd411]
Balance all coin-counting machines in dual control at least weekly. If an employee completes a buy/sell transaction after balancing and before Perm Off, rebalance the cashline on TellerView.
Ensure all employees complete their buy/sell transactions on TellerView before balancing.
Obtain the balancing information from the coin-counting machine in dual control.
Sign on to the appropriate coin-counting machine cashline on TellerView in dual control.
Select T614 Balancing.
Enter each coin denomination amount. Do not enter a lump sum.
Select Daily in the Counting For drop-down menu and enter the balancing information.

[PR63-c5abe51162a5]
Determine if the cashline balances:Balances or has a difference of $3 or less: Select Ok. Does not balance: TellerView automatically navigates to the cash difference transaction. Follow the steps in the Balancing: Drop and Go, Teller, and Vault > Process a cash difference on TellerView procedure.
Complete a Perm Off when balancing is complete.

## Random monthly cash count

[PR63-e0c4207b5185]
Follow requirements for random monthly cash counts in the Cash Counts and Random Monthly Cash Detail > Unannounced random monthly cash count procedure.
Obtain the balancing information from the machine in dual control.
Sign on to the appropriate coin-counting machine cashline on TellerView in dual control.
Select T614 Balancing.
Enter each coin denomination amount (not a lump sum).
Select Shared in the Counting For drop-down menu and enter the balancing information.
Select Random Monthly in the Reason for Count drop-down menu.

[PR63-19f81fdc5b74]
Determine if the cashline balances:Balances or has a difference $3 or less: Go to the next step. Does not balance: If the difference cannot be resolved:Complete the File Out of Balance screen to process the difference. In the Comments field, provide details to explain the difference, if known, using acronyms in the Balancing: Drop and Go, Teller, and Vault > Process a cash difference procedure.
Follow the system prompts to enter dual control credentials and submit the balance.
Complete a Perm Off when complete.

## Monthly testing

[PR63-f87320e2edc7]
The employee assigned to complete the single control Coin-Counting Machine Testing Control Online task must complete the monthly testing before the coin machine is used in the morning or just before balancing.
Obtain the following coin from either an employee cashline or the vault cashline:

Table columns: Denomination | # of rolls | Total in $ | Total number of units

[PR63-2f204210c633]

[PR63-0556abe6bcbe]

[PR63-36ceb4100757]
Denomination: $0.25 | # of rolls: 1 | Total in $: $10.00 | Total number of units: 40

[PR63-d0880da1367f]

[PR63-02f0224ded92]

[PR63-8995da42e0b5]
Denomination: Total | Total in $: $36.00 | Total number of units: 390 units

[PR63-e20588eb6f47]
Open and hand count each roll to ensure the contents are correct.
Complete a T611 Buy Sell Cash to sell the coin from the cashline that provided the coin to the coin-counting machine cashline.
Run the coin through the machine and confirm the machine displays the correct amounts:Correct amount displays: Complete the Control Online Coin-Counting Machine Testing task. Correct amount does not display: Call Cummins Allison at 1-877-319-1159 to schedule servicing to recalibrate the machine. Enter a task comment stating the test failed and the date of the scheduled servicing prior to completing the Control Online Coin-Counting Machine Testing task.
