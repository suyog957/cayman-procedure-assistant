### 00:49 — Follow the evidence through the system
The architecture has two paths. Offline preparation parses the eleven original HTML files, builds traceable passages, and creates lexical and semantic indexes. At runtime, a single orchestrator resolves the question, verifies the snapshot, retrieves and reranks passages, and gives bounded evidence to the local generator. Output checks determine whether to release, correct, or withhold the draft. Retrieval augmented generation was chosen because procedural knowledge needs inspectable sources and explicit updates.

### 01:15 — Keep the rule with its conditions
The inputs contain narrative instructions and one hundred thirty-nine HTML tables, including nested tables. Structural parsing preserves headings, column labels, row conditions, and source locations. Table continuations repeat their conditions. Each passage carries its procedure, section, publication text, original file hash, and passage identity. Nearby qualifications can be included during evidence assembly. Linked external procedures are not fetched. This reduces lost context, but it does not guarantee that every relevant exception is retrieved.

### 01:42 — Exact terms plus semantic meaning
Hybrid retrieval combines two complementary signals. BM twenty-five matches exact terms, procedure identifiers, and specialized wording. MiniLM embeddings retrieve semantic paraphrases. Reciprocal rank fusion combines their rankings, and a cross encoder reranks the candidates jointly with the question. The generator usually receives up to three nonduplicate passages within a bounded evidence budget. Exact local vector search is sufficient for this small collection.

### 03:46 — Inspect each statement's supporting evidence
The Checks view separates the release decision into concrete screens. Citation labels must exist, factual statements need attribution, and numbers must occur in their own cited passages. Recognized strict and inclusive thresholds are compared as well. Each screened statement has a source association and a supporting evidence window. Direct wording can use a verbatim match; paraphrases use a natural language inference classifier. These checks can miss errors or reject valid answers. Checks passed means the implemented screens passed, not that correctness has been independently established.

### 11:41 — Evaluate the employee task, not just the text
The next validation needs independent operational reviewers. Accuracy asks whether the guidance and conditions are correct for the situation. Relevance asks whether it answers the actual task. Compare employee task completion and time to correct guidance against existing search, while measuring both unsupported answers and unnecessary refusals.
